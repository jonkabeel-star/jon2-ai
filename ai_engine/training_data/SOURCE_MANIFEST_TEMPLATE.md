## Mandatory rule

Until every relevant field is supported by evidence and a human reviewer explicitly records APPROVED for the intended use, the source must remain quarantined and must not be downloaded into the training corpus or used for model training. A dataset card, public availability, or packaging licence alone is not sufficient proof of rights to underlying text.
